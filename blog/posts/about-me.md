I'm a Solutions Architect and Tech Lead at OneBlinc, where I spend my days on Python, Django and AWS, building fintech products for the US market. I've been writing software professionally for about ten years, and I got here by being wrong about what I'd enjoy more than once.

Case in point - I used to think cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity were the boring parts of this job. Someone else's problem. Then I actually had to own them, and it turns out they're the parts I like most. Architecture decisions, network boundaries, blast radius, who can touch what and why: that's the work I'd pick on a Friday afternoon. Meanwhile, mobile development and I have quietly agreed to see other people.

I studied Computer Science at UNICAMP, and before that I did the integrated high school + technical program at COTIL. I've gone back there a few times as an invited speaker to give workshops to high school and technical students, which is still one of my favorite things I get to do. Explaining something to someone who is fifteen and skeptical is the best test of whether you actually understand it.

Two things I'm chasing right now:

- **Showing up more locally.** I'm speaking at DjangoCon US 2026 in Chicago, but I want to be more present in the community closer to home - meetups, local events, the kind of thing that doesn't require a plane ticket.
- **The golden jacket.** All twelve AWS certifications. I'm partway there and fully aware of how that sounds. I'm doing it anyway.

## What this blog is for

Mostly a place to write things down instead of losing them in a Slack thread. Expect notes on cloud and AWS architecture, Django and backend engineering, security and DevSecOps, and the occasional career or community post - talks I gave, certifications I studied for, mistakes I'd rather you not repeat.

No schedule, no content calendar. Just posts when I have something worth saying.

Thanks for reading - see you in the next one.
